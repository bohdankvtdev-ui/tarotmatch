Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Tarot Match (“the app”) is a memory matching game that uses tarot card art. It does not provide fortune-telling or paid readings. This policy describes what data is stored on your device and what advertising partners may collect.

Who we are

Tarot Match is published as an iOS app (bundle ID bohdankvtdev@gmail.com). Privacy questions: bohdankvtdev@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Game progress stays on your iPhone or iPad: scores, streaks, levels, and how well you remember each card. This data is not uploaded to our servers. We do not create an account. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Advertising

The app shows Google AdMob banner ads at the top and bottom of Home, Game, and Deck screens. Google may collect device identifiers and advertising data to deliver ads, measure them, and fight fraud. If you allow tracking on iOS, ads may be personalized. If you decline, we request non-personalized ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google’s consent form may appear before ads are personalized. You can open ad privacy choices from About in the app. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

iOS may ask for permission to use the advertising identifier. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Tarot Match is not directed at children under 13 and is not a Kids Category app.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking on iOS and still play with non-personalized ads.
  • Use system settings to limit ad tracking.
  • Uninstall the app to delete local game data.
  • Email us at bohdankvtdev@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you have read the revised policy.
