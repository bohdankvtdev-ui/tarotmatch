Support

Tarot Match is a local memory matching game. There is no account. Write to us: bohdankvtdev@gmail.com

How do I play?

Open the app and tap Play. Flip two cards. Match the same tarot identity to clear the board. Levels add more pairs.

What is Deck?

Deck is a journal of cards you have matched and how familiar they are. Progress is stored only on this device.

Do you give readings?

No. The app does not predict the future, sell readings, or connect you to a reader. It is a memory puzzle that uses tarot card identities.

Ads

Two banner ads (top and bottom) support the free game. On iPhone you can decline tracking and still play. In some regions a consent form appears first. Change ad choices from About → Ad privacy in the app.

I lost my progress

Scores and the deck journal live on that iPhone or iPad. Uninstalling, switching devices, or clearing the app’s data starts a new journal. We cannot restore a run from our side.

Age and content

Some tarot images (for example Death, Devil, Tower) are traditional cards. The game is not made for the Kids category.
