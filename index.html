TAROT MATCH

Flip two cards. Remember the art. Match The Moon, the courts, and every pip in a full 78-card deck. A calm memory puzzle that teaches the cards as you play.

App Store Support Privacy

How it plays

Classic memory match, tuned to the tarot — not a reading app.

Match

Two cards at a time. Pair the same identity to clear the board and climb levels.

Learn

A full 78-card structure: 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana across four suits.

Remember

Cards you miss come back. A Deck journal keeps what you have learned on this device.

Find the pair

A tiny board with two pairs. Tap a card, then find its twin.

From the deck

Pixel tarot art used in the game — majors, courts, and the card back.

On the App Store

Free to play, supported by two banner ads. Tracking is optional. Progress stays on your device. The app does not predict the future.
